FAQ

Pricing Notes

Are the prices fixed?

No. Published prices are starting rates or illustrative estimates. Final pricing depends on the agreed deliverables, complexity, footage, and volume.

Can I request simple editing instead of advanced effects?

Yes. We can recommend a simpler editing approach when advanced motion graphics or effects are not needed.

Can I combine different services?

Yes. You can combine video editing, thumbnails, repurposing, podcast editing, and branding to suit your needs.

Do monthly packages get custom pricing?

Monthly packages can be tailored to your content volume and production needs. Any discount is agreed in the quote.

How many revisions are included?

Up to 3 revisions per deliverable.

What are your turnaround times?

Turnaround times will be discussed while quoting.

What are your payment terms?

50% advance payment before work begins, with the remaining 50% due upon completion and before final files are delivered. Payments are accepted via PayPal.

What if I need something outside my package?

Any additional services or deliverables outside the agreed scope are quoted separately. We confirm the final deliverables, pricing, and turnaround time before work begins.
